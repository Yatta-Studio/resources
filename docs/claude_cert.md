Yes, there are several free practice tests and sample question sets available online for the Claude Certified Developer – Foundations (CCDV-F) exam. [1, 2] 
Because this certification focuses heavily on real-world development decisions (like when to use the Messages API vs. Message Batches, managing rate limits, or building Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers), working through scenario-based questions is highly recommended. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5] 
The most prominent free resources include:
## 1. Free Online Samplers & Mock Drills

* 
* [FlashGenius CCDV-F Practice Test](https://flashgenius.net/sample-tests/ccdv-f): Offers a comprehensive free practice test broken down across all 8 official exam domains (including Applications, Agents, and Claude Code). The questions are scenario-based and mirror the design choices you'll face on the real test. [1, 2] 
* [Tutorials Dojo Free Sampler](https://portal.tutorialsdojo.com/product/free-claude-certified-developer-foundations-ccdv-f-practice-exams-sampler/): Provides a 20-question free sampler that you can take in Timed Mode (to simulate the 120-minute test pacing) or Review Mode (which gives you immediate explanations and bonus flashcards). [3] 
* [Preporato CCDV-F Sample Questions](https://preporato.com/blog/ccdv-f-practice-questions-with-explanations-2026): Includes a free block of 20 realistic, multi-choice, and "Select TWO" format questions mapped exactly to Anthropic’s official domain weightings, complete with detailed answers and rationales. [6]

## 2. Community & Instructor Resources

## Core Areas to Expect in Practice Questions
No matter which platform you test yourself on, ensure your practice covers these heavy-hitting blueprint areas:

* 
* Applications & Integration (33.1% of the exam): Using the Messages API, handling streaming responses, token counting, and deploying Message Batches.
* Model Selection & Optimization (16.8% of the exam): Balancing cost vs. latency, configuring prompt caching, and utilizing extended thinking.
* Agents & Workflows (14.7% of the exam): Building agent loops, orchestration patterns, and tool use. [1, 3, 9]
